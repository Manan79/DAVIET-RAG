The Department of CSE AI & ML is a part of the DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology. The Department was established in 2022. We offers program leading to the Bachelor of CSE in Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning. We are a growing department with an outstanding faculty, keeping pace with the rapid proliferation of, and advances in, computing and information technology. Our commitments to quality undergraduate teaching and strong research programs provide a solid foundation for those seeking to become well prepared, talented, and indispensable computing professionals. Approximately 120 full time undergraduates pursuing their degree in CSE AI/ML. The departmental faculty actively participates and provides leadership to major research centers on campus. Opportunities to pursue faculty supervised undergraduate research and project work are readily available The department makes extensive use of wideband connectivity to the commodity Internet Students and faculty have ready access to advanced computational hardware and software both through Institute resources as well as through our more specialized laboratory workstations. The Bachelor of CSE with a major in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning is accredited by the, All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), a specialized accrediting body recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation.
