ECE Alumni
Ever increasing pace of development in electronics has made an Electronics Engineer a catalyst for the change of the modern society. The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering has been consistently working towards the goal to produce highly skilled and scientifically oriented manpower through flexible, adaptive and progressive training programs and a cohesive interaction among the research organizations, academicians and industries.
The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering was established in the year 2001 with an intake of 60 in graduate program and currently department is having an annual intake of 120. Department is also running PG program – M.Tech in Electronics & Communication with intake of 18.
Department has consistently maintained an exemplary academic record. The greatest asset of the department is its highly motivated and learned faculty, with a strong vision & mission to nurture the students with core and multidisciplinary field with academic excellence, placement and simultaneously inculcating core values to the learners & to educate students in order to produce competent technocrats for professional carrier, higher studies and enable the students to develop skills to solve complex technological problems of current time.
The department has faculty members having vast experience in their field. The faculty has succeeded in keeping a lively atmosphere among the students, with timely up gradations in the curriculum and innovative teaching techniques. The staff members are deputed to participate in workshops, conferences, symposia and refresher courses to keep in pace with recent developments in the field of Electronics & Communications Engineering. The available diversity of expertise of the faculty with the support of the other staff prepares the students to work in global multicultural environment.
We hope that we will continue to deliver our best to serve the society and mankind. It is also expected that our students will continue to pass on the skills which they have developed during their stay at this department to whole of the world for a better society. We will be happy to receive suggestions for further improvement and development of the department.

Faculty
The ECE has been largely successful as it has been very fortunate in having at its disposal, a dedicated team of technopreneurs that is always prepare to brave the odds and bring innovation in the class rooms and labs. Under the Head leadership of Dr. Neeru Malhotra, HOD, ECE, faculty combines in itself the power of technical expertise and youthful energy capable of helping, guiding and training the students. The excellent results produced during the past eight year bear testimony to the hard work being put in by the faculty along with it the faculty has been continuously engaged in faculty improvement program.
