Welcome to DAV Institute of Engineering & Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, a pioneering institution in the realm of technical education. Established in 2001 under the aegis of the DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC), New Delhi, which manages a chain of more than 970 institutions in India and abroad, DAVIET stands as a beacon of excellence in engineering, technology, and professional education. Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated with I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University, Jalandhar, DAVIET is the first degree level engineering institute established by the Dayanand Anglo Vedic College Managing Committee, New Delhi.
Situated in the heart of Jalandhar city on an expansive 18 acre campus adjacent to DAV College, DAVIET boasts a picturesque landscape with a running canal at its rear and the historic Burlton Park at its forefront. Our institute fosters original research and technological advancements at the forefront of innovation.
At DAVIET, we are committed to nurturing competent, motivated engineers and scientists through outcome based education. We strongly emphasize imparting training that cultivates technical prowess and instils human values and environmental consciousness. We pride ourselves in the collaborations and MOUs with leading institutions in the world namely the University of Ontario, The Institute of Technology, Canada, and the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar, DAVIET is committed to providing quality education and world class exposure to all our students. With an exemplary track record in placements, DAVIET has forged alliances with over 200 esteemed companies for internships and job placements. In the past two years alone, more than 700 of our students have secured internships or employment opportunities. Notably, one of our B.Tech students recently clinched an international placement with a remarkable pay package of 70 LPA, a testament to the quality of education and training imparted at DAVIET.
We take pride in our numerous accolades, including the IKG Punjab Technical University Youth Festival Trophy, which we have won an unprecedented 23 times. DAVIET also serves as an excellent hub for incubation and entrepreneurship, fostering a culture of innovation and enterprise among our students.
Our institute boasts state of the art facilities and centers of excellence, including the Microsoft Certified Campus, Texas Instruments Innovation Lab, IBM Academy, Huawei Academy and CISCO Networking Academy, among others. These collaborations equip our students with hands on experience and industry relevant skills, ensuring their readiness for the dynamic professional landscape.
At DAVIET, research is at the forefront of our endeavors, with proactive initiatives spanning a multitude of disciplines, from lightwave communication and robotics to organic chemistry and computational intelligence. Our faculty and students are engaged in cutting edge research that addresses contemporary challenges and contributes to societal advancement.
Join us at DAVIET, where academic excellence, innovation, and societal impact converge to shape the engineers and technologists of tomorrow. Discover a vibrant learning community that celebrates diversity, fosters creativity, and empowers individuals to realize their fullest potential.tjal.org/about/
