Considered as one of the oldest Engineering discipline, the profession deals with the wide variety of engineering task involving, planning, design & execution of the infrastructural facilities viz, building, dams, tunnels, highways, harbours, etc.Established in the year 2002 the Civil Engineering department is a community of 240 students, 10 full time faculty members, several visiting and adjunct faculty members and lab staff working together. The students/alumni of the department are serving the government, public & private sector in top positions. There is 100% placement of the students of the department.
Civil engineering department is fully equipped with ultra modern lab facilities which provides students an opportunity to work on latest equipment and to develop their computational abilities in analysis, design and execution of Civil engineering works.
| Geotechnical Engineering Lab: had the facilities of computerized triaxial shear test and direct shear test, SPT, etc. | Transportation Engineering lab: had all the latest equipment for testing of all kinds of highway materials. |
| | |
| Structural Analysis Lab: helps in understanding the basic theorems of stresses and moments in various structures through models. | Surveying Lab: introduces our students to latest digital and electronics devices i.e electronic theodolites and Total stations, etc |
| Fluid Mechanics Lab: facilitates our students to understand the mechanics of fluid flow in a better way through well designed models and equipment. | Concrete Testing Lab: is equipped with 2000KN Compression Testing machine, flexural testing machine. |
| Strength of Materials lab: is having 600KN capacity universal testing machine Fatigue testing machine and other machines to test the various properties of steel. | CAD Lab: is equipped with 30 work stations loaded with software likeAuto CAD, STADD Pro and other construction management software. |
