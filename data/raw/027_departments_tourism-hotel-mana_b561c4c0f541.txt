DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology is a premier educational Institution of North India, providing quality education to aspiring technocrats, managers, entrepreneurs and professionals. It is managed by DAV College Managing Committee which has over 130 years experience in the field of education. It has 30,000,00 active students currently enrolled in more than 850 colleges and schools across the country and 40,000,00 alumni serving the society in various capacities. DAVIET has carved out a niche for itself owing to the strong academia industry interface with the emphasis on promoting original research to make the students proficient in both technical and practical dimensions of their respective fields. Keeping in mind the multi dimensional nature of problems and their interdisciplinary solutions, DAVIET has decided to diversify and offer new courses in addition to the already existing programs.
Department of Hospitality and tourism was established in 2019 to offer Bachelor of hospitality and tourism The department maintains an excellent teaching and learning environment with dedicated, qualified and dynamic faculty members and well equipped laboratories. The core competencies of the department include food production, food and beverage service, front office and housekeeping etc. The students of our department take up careers in various computing industries and large hotel companies, start up companies, management companies and other government based research organizations.
Department of hospitality and tourism balances the practical and theoretical aspects and provide students with a background for professional employment and also to pursue further studies in the discipline. This course is design so that one can give the best hospitality to the guest/customer.
DEPARTMENTAL LIBRARY
In addition to main library, a Departmental Library has also been set up which has books covering diverse research areas. Our departmental library has front office, food production, food and beverage service, housekeeping books which a student can go and take the advantage of.
