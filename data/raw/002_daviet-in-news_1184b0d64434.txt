DAVIET STUDENTS WIN HACKATHON WITH SHAREMESH INNOVATION
Students of DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, have brought laurels to the institution by winning ₹ 11000 at a prestigious 30 hour offline hackathon organised in Jalandhar. DAVIET CELEBRATES SHWETA'S DOUBLE SUCCESS IN INDIAN AIR FORCE SELECTION
DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, takes immense pride in celebrating the remarkable achievement of Shweta from the Department of Computer Science Engineering , who has successfully cleared the Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT) and earned recommendations in two successive selection attempts, first on 13 February and subsequently on 5 June. She is currently undergoing training at the Indian Air Force Academy, Hyderabad, and upon successful completion of her training will be commissioned as an officer in the Indian Air Force. Congratulating Shweta, Dr Sandeep Vij, Director cum Principal , stated that her accomplishment exemplifies perseverance, self discipline and an unwavering commitment to excellence. He observed that such achievements inspire fellow students to pursue ambitious career aspirations and bring distinction to the institution. Lt Jaswinder Singh Dhillon also congratulated Shweta, appreciating her dedication, determination and commitment towards serving the nation. He expressed confidence that she would uphold the highest standards of professionalism and excellence in her future responsibilities. The institute congratulates Shweta on this significant milestone and wishes her a successful training period and a distinguished career in the Indian Air Force.
