Welcome to the Department of Mechanical Engineering at DAV Institute of Engineering & Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar. Established in 2003, our department has become a cornerstone of innovation and excellence within the institute, dedicated to shaping the future of mechanical engineering through rigorous education, research, and practical application. The Department of Mechanical Engineering at DAV Institute of Engineering & Technology, Jalandhar, is dedicated to nurturing the next generation of mechanical engineers who will drive innovation and progress in the field. With our strong academic programs, advanced facilities, and commitment to research and industry engagement, we are proud to contribute to the educational and professional landscape of mechanical engineering.
Our Vision & Mission
M 1: To impart quality cum value based technical education aimed at enhancing technical skills of the students leading to excellence in research, innovation and creative competence resulting in effectively responding to the needs of the society/industry.
M 2: To prepare professionally competent engineers having academic and entrepreneurial leadership acumen coupled with effective communication skills, teamwork, professional ethics and global awareness required for engineering profession and society in general.
Academic Programs
We offer a undergraduate program designed to equip students with a robust understanding of mechanical engineering principles and practices. Our curriculum is continuously updated to reflect the latest industry trends and technological advancements, ensuring that our graduates are well prepared for the challenges of the modern engineering landscape.
Facilities and Resources
Our department boasts state of the art laboratories and facilities that provide students with hands on experience in various aspects of mechanical engineering. These include advanced labs in Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Heat Transfer, Manufacturing Processes, and CAD/CAM. We also offer access to cutting edge software and equipment to support research and practical learning.
Research and Innovation
We encourage a culture of research and innovation within our department. Our faculty and students are actively involved in research projects that address contemporary engineering problems and contribute to technological advancements. We collaborate with industry partners and other research institutions to ensure that our work remains relevant and impactful.
Industry Connections and Career Opportunities
Our strong connections with the industry provide students with valuable internship opportunities and exposure to real world engineering challenges. We organize workshops, seminars, and guest lectures featuring experts from various sectors to enhance students' learning experiences and prepare them for successful careers.
Community and Extracurricular Activities
Beyond academics, we believe in the holistic development of our students. Our department supports a range of extracurricular activities, including technical clubs, engineering competitions, and cultural events, fostering teamwork, leadership, and personal growth.
