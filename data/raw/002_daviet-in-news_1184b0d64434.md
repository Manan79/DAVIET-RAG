#### **DAVIET Hosts Distinguished Alumna Ms Rubeen Gill for an Expert Talk on Technology Leadership**
####
As part of its Silver Jubilee Celebrations, DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, organised an expert talk by its distinguished alumna, **Ms Rubeen Gill, Senior Technical Program Manager at Mastercard**. The session was titled _“From Software Engineering to Strategic Technology Leadership.”**Dr Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal, DAVIET** , appreciated the participation of the distinguished alumna and stated that accomplished alumni serve as role models for students. He observed that interactions with successful professionals provide valuable industry exposure, practical insights and inspiration, helping students make informed career choices._
#### **DAVIET Strengthens Industry–Academia Ties: Corporate Leaders from Samco International and MCK Impex Explore Collaborative Opportunities**
####
DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, recently hosted an interaction with two distinguished industry representatives, Mr. Sanjeev Juneja, Managing Director, Samco International and Chairman, IIF Chandigarh Chapter, and Mr. Manish Kwatra, Director, MCK IMPEX, Jalandhar and Secretary, IIF Chandigarh Chapter. The interaction focused on exploring collaborative opportunities between the institute and the corporate sector to strengthen professional training, experiential learning and student employability. **Dr. Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal,** DAVIET, emphasised that industry–academia collaboration plays a pivotal role in strengthening the quality and relevance of technical education.
#### **DAVIET Celebrates Vishwakarma Day with Traditional Pooja Ceremony**
####
The Department of Mechanical Engineering at DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, celebrated Vishwakarma Day on 10th November 2026 with devotion and traditional fervour. A special Pooja Ceremony was organised in the Turning Shop of the Mechanical Engineering Department Laboratory to mark the auspicious occasion. The celebration paid tribute to Lord Vishwakarma, revered as the divine architect and the patron deity of craftsmen, engineers and artisans. The ceremony was graced by Dr Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal, DAVIET, whose presence added significance to the occasion.
#### **Congratulations to all**
**DAVIET STUDENTS WIN HACKATHON WITH SHAREMESH INNOVATION**

Students of DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, have brought laurels to the institution by winning **₹ 11000** at a prestigious 30-hour offline hackathon organised in Jalandhar. Competing against more than 150 teams comprising over 500 participants from various colleges and universities, the DAVIET team secured recognition for its innovative project, **ShareMesh**. The hackathon provided a competitive platform for young innovators to demonstrate their technical abilities through coding, hardware prototyping and live presentations before an expert jury. With a total prize pool exceeding ₹1,00,000, the event recognised outstanding technological solutions through cash prizes, gadgets, trophies and certificates. **Dr. Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal, DAVIET** , congratulated the students on their achievement and stated that their success reflected the institution’s commitment to nurturing innovation, technical excellence and an entrepreneurial mindset.
**DAVIET CELEBRATES SHWETA’S DOUBLE SUCCESS IN INDIAN AIR FORCE SELECTION**
DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, takes immense pride in celebrating the remarkable achievement of **Shweta** from the **Department of Computer Science Engineering** , who has successfully cleared the **Air Force Common Admission Test (AFCAT)** and earned recommendations in two successive selection attempts, first on 13 February and subsequently on 5 June. She is currently undergoing training at the Indian Air Force Academy, Hyderabad, and upon successful completion of her training will be commissioned as an officer in the Indian Air Force. Congratulating Shweta, **Dr Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal** , stated that her accomplishment exemplifies perseverance, self-discipline and an unwavering commitment to excellence. He observed that such achievements inspire fellow students to pursue ambitious career aspirations and bring distinction to the institution. Lt Jaswinder Singh Dhillon also congratulated Shweta, appreciating her dedication, determination and commitment towards serving the nation. He expressed confidence that she would uphold the highest standards of professionalism and excellence in her future responsibilities. The institute congratulates Shweta on this significant milestone and wishes her a successful training period and a distinguished career in the Indian Air Force.
**DAVIET WOMEN’S CHESS TEAM RETAINS IKG PTU TITLE; MEN’S TEAM SECURES SECOND RUNNER-UP POSITION**

The Women’s Chess Team of DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, has brought distinction to the institute by winning the IKG PTU Inter-College Chess Championship for the second consecutive year. The Men’s Chess Team further enhanced the institute’s sporting achievements by securing the Second Runner-Up position in the tournament. The championship witnessed commendable performances by both teams, reflecting their strategic ability, concentration and competitive spirit. **Dr Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal,** stated that the achievement reflects DAVIET’s commitment to holistic education and the development of students beyond the classroom. He emphasised that sports play a vital role in nurturing discipline, analytical thinking, resilience and leadership qualities. Dr Jaswinder Singh Dhillon, President Sports, appreciated the players for their dedication, perseverance and consistent performance.
**DAVIET ORGANISES BADGE DISTRIBUTION CEREMONY FOR NEWLY APPOINTED CLASS REPRESENTATIVES**

DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, organised a badge distribution ceremony to honour its newly appointed Class Representatives (CRs). The ceremony commenced with a warm welcome to the Principal and the Deans. Addressing the gathering, the **Director-cum-Principal Dr Sandeep Vij** congratulated the newly appointed representatives and highlighted their role as a vital link between students and faculty.
**DAVIET EMPOWERS POSTAL OFFICERS THROUGH BUSINESS AND MARKETING SKILLS TRAINING**

DAV Institute of Engineering and Technology’s Centre for Corporate Training (DAVIET- CCT), successfully organised two (3-day each) Capacity Building Programmes on “Business and Marketing Skills for Middle Management Officers” for Inspectors of Posts (IPs), Assistant Superintendents of Posts (ASPOs) and Postal Services Group ‘B’ officers from the Department of Posts. The programme aimed to strengthen managerial competencies through contemporary marketing practices, digital strategies and artificial intelligence (AI)-driven tools for improved public service delivery. Addressing the participants, **Dr. Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal, DAVIET** , emphasised the need for public-sector organisations to embrace technological advancement and customer-centric governance. He highlighted that the effective integration of data analytics, generative AI and digital tools into routine operations can improve service efficiency, strengthen public trust and foster responsive, citizen-focused administration. He further underscored the importance of continuous learning, adaptability and strategic thinking in enabling middle-management officers to respond effectively to evolving public expectations.
#### DAVIET EXPLORES INTERDISCIPLINARY APPLICATIONS OF MATHEMATICS THROUGH EXPERT LECTURE
####
The Research, Innovation & Consultancy (RIC) Cell of DAV Institute of Engineering & Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, in collaboration with the Department of Applied Sciences, organised an expert lecture on “Applications of Mathematics in Emerging Areas of Science, Technology, and Management.” The session featured Dr. Gagan Deep, Associate Professor and Head, PG Department of Mathematics, Hans Raj Mahila Maha Vidyalaya, Jalandhar, as the keynote speaker. The programme brought faculty members together to examine the relevance of mathematical concepts in contemporary research, technological progress and professional practice. Dr. Sandeep Vij, Director-cum-Principal, welcomed Dr. Gagan Deep and acknowledged her efforts in sharing her academic expertise with the faculty. He emphasised that mathematical thinking is essential to understanding complex challenges in science, engineering, technology and management.
#### DAVIET Organises Expert Talk on Blockchain Technology

The Department of Business Management at DAV Institute of Engineering & Technology (DAVIET), Jalandhar, organised an expert talk on “Blockchain Technology: Applications Shaping the Digital Age”. Dr. Sanjeev Gupta, Professor, School of Commerce and Management, Central University of Himachal Pradesh, was the resource person for the session. The programme witnessed the participation of 100 students and 13 faculty members, providing an academic platform to explore the growing relevance of blockchain technology in contemporary business and digital ecosystems.
