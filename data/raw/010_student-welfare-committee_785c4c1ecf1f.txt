Student Welfare Committee
Objectives:
It shall be the function of the committee to consider and recommend policies relating to the coordination and regulation of student organizations, social events and other related activities; advisory and counseling programs; health services; financial aid including loans, scholarships, fee concessions etc.; student housing orientation and new student programs; student development programs; sponsorship to students for conferences etc.. It shall also strive to protect the rights of students. It shall be responsibility of the committee to maintain and improve an atmosphere conductively to the overall development of students.
Following 'Student Welfare Committee' of the Institute is hereby constituted for a period of 02 years as under:
