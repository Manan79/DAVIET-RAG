About the department
Founded in 2007, the Department of Business Management is the center for quality education in the field of management. Transnational and cut throat competition are demanding immense transformations in the corporate world. Rapid upgradation of technology and major social changes all over the world are other issues which pose challenge as well as an opportunity at the same time. But ultimately it is the human power and persistence that have to cope up with ever demanding situations. Keeping this in view maximum focus is required for enhancing skills and abilities of the persons for producing furnished professionals. We at DAVIET believe that today's business leaders need to pose an excellent package of specialized skills, positive attitude, analytical orientation to be able to handle complex business situations. Education and training programs are revolving around this philosophy in the Department of Business Management. The department is facilitated with best state of art infrastructure and dedicated faculty to act as mentors providing directions for individual development.
